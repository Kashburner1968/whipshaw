## What changed

**Credit:** the quoted high-yield OAS increased **0.09 percentage points (9 basis points)** from October 13 to October 14. The latest listed reading is a **dated daily observation**, not an intraday credit quote. An increase means greater compensation demanded for bearing high-yield credit risk, but it does not identify individual bond trades or predict a specific equity-index move.

**Energy:** September consumer and producer fuel-price increases are unusually large in the cited releases. Energy price pressure can pass through to transportation and household budgets, but the pass-through rate differs across sectors.

**Demand:** headline retail sales were stronger than the narrower gasoline-excluded and control-group measures. All three are nominal; they should not be described as real consumption growth.

**Cross-asset:** oil, Treasury yields and copper quotes above come from different timestamps. The comparison is useful as a watchlist, not as a synchronized causal test.
