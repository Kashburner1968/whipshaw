This has happened before

1987 — Black Monday

The Federal Reserve pledged liquidity support after the crash. Market functioning stabilized before the underlying shock had vanished.

1998 — Long-Term Capital Management

The Federal Reserve facilitated a private rescue that interrupted a disorderly fire sale before leverage and fragility disappeared.

2008 — The Global Financial Crisis

Emergency facilities, near-zero rates and asset purchases arrived as the system broke. The market received enormous support. The economy still fell into the Great Recession.

2020 — The Pandemic Crisis

The Federal Reserve bought Treasuries, mortgage securities and corporate credit while opening emergency facilities. Market functioning recovered much faster than the physical economy. The screen healed before the world did.

The lesson is simple: intervention can silence a market warning before it repairs the conditions that produced the warning.
